# Incident log collection checklist

- Capture the smallest time window that answers the diagnostic question.
- Record service and software versions and timestamps in UTC.
- Remove credentials, player IPs, UUIDs, chat, and unrelated personal data.
- Preserve relevant stack traces and causal context after redaction.
- Store the bundle in an access-controlled location with an expiry.
- Confirm shared links and temporary copies are revoked after the investigation.
